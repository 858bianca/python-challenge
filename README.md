# Python-Scripting
PyBank - Python Script to analyze financial records

My task for this anaylisis was to take the dataset budget_data.csv and reorganize it to calcualte the total number of months, net total amount and change for "Profit/Losses", as well calculate the average change within "Profits/Losses" throughout the entregrity of the data and retreive back the greatest increase and decrease of profits. 

To complete this calculation the dataset was composed of two columns: "Date" and "Profit/Losses". The first calculation was to determine the total number of months present in the given dataset. Onced calculated the next step was to calculate the net total of Profit/Losses which sums up the profit/losses through the entrie period. The average change was then calculated to determine the change in profits/losses overtime. Finally the greatest increase and decrease was than determined to identify the greatest increase and decrease in profit within the period,

At the end of the python script it should effectively provide insights to profit and loss trends within the budget_data.csv file.
